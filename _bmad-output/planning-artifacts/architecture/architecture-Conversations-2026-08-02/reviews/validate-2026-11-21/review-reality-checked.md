# Reality check · 21 November 2026

**Verdict — revise the handoff before using it to implement recovery or qualify a host.** The November section accurately describes the open pilot gates. Inherited decisions still leave readers with competing answers about valid replay history, what a Healthy checkpoint proves, and which deployment owns Dapr Components. Each has a specific source or owner decision that the handoff can cite.

I reviewed the November working-copy spine at Conversations `08953f5eed404228a2b8bd41c7d2bf5eaaab543a`, the recorded root gitlinks, the refreshed owner checkouts, source, tests, approved story specifications and retained evidence. Findings below concern the architecture handoff. This review made no new runtime, package, provider or deployment qualification.

## High findings

1. **Replay validity differs between the domain and projection.** AD-8 binds duplicate identity to the exact sequence as well as the payload and selected time. The domain verifier accepts a repeated `ParticipantAdded` or `ConversationProjectChanged` identity at a later sequence. Its tests expect replay to succeed. The projection includes sequence in the binding, uses one identity map for contract event IDs and position-only message IDs, and rejects a changed binding before publishing the candidate generation. Identical input can therefore receive different validity decisions. Reachability through the production event producer and what existing readers should observe after rejection remain open. **Discuss** under retrospective item 52 with the Domain and Projection owners. Identify the existing projection rule and the two domain exceptions in the current spine. ADR 0005 explicitly excludes this decision. 2. **The authorization guarantee ends at the last proven checkpoint.** The November text calls the tenant projection fail-closed. A missing, gapped, corrupt or unavailable snapshot denies access. A durable Healthy snapshot grants from its recorded membership with no age check. A revocation whose delivery has stalled can leave the previous grant active. This distinction matters for the supported host and for interpreting degraded telemetry. Story 16.1 explicitly selected this staleness policy and unlimited retries. Action 54 records the consequence and the handoff correction. **Clarify** the checkpoint guarantee and its dependency on subscription progress. Keep tenant-revocation lag separate from the conversation freshness meter in 16.5. 3. **Component ownership changes between the Conversations pin and the refreshed Platform checkout.** At the Platform gitlink in Conversations `08953f5e`, the application chart owns Components. At refreshed Platform `abb62da8`, the chart emits zero Components and the environment writer renders them. The revision-specific sentence in the spine is accurate. A deployment following only the November handoff cannot tell which procedure applies to the root candidate. **Clarify** both revisions and require the deployment owner to identify the adopted revision during 9.3 qualification.
