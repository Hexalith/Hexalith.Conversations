# Independent good-spine review · 21 November 2026

**Verdict — changes needed before the spine can serve as the single initiative handoff.** Three high-severity findings affect which contract a team follows, whether a tenant authorization checkpoint establishes freshness, and which histories can be rebuilt. The November supplement accurately describes the open pilot work. This review covers the effective initiative architecture at Conversations `08953f5e`, including inherited decisions, its canonical SPEC, the November PRD and sprint supplement, the current-change policy, ADRs and source. Findings concern the technical handoff. Product execution and release approval have their own gates.

## High severity

**R1. The canonical SPEC still selects the wrong authority.** Its sole capability instructs consumers to select V15, reports AR-15 blocked on an external validator and bars Epic 16 entry. The inherited AD-3 selects the last complete marker, which yields V22. A reader reaching the November supplement instead finds routine work under current-change policy, a historical hold, and completed Stories 16.1–16.3 under scoped waivers. The spec’s unqualified instruction can restart a retired process or conceal the technical decisions that follow the final marker. The archived marker and frontmatter have historical value. **Disposition — reconcile.** Have the spec owner point its discovery capability to the current planning section and the runbook’s historical boundary. Give agents entering through the architecture folder an explicit first-read link to that section. Preserve the frozen bytes and the historical hold. **R2. A Healthy checkpoint has two meanings when the Tenants feed stalls.** The inherited rule requires denial on lagged or rolled-back tenant state, and AD-11 depends on the fail-closed projection. Story 16.1 defined staleness as a missing or unproven record. Its production mapping takes an internally Healthy checkpoint as healthy without proving its distance from the source. A previously permitted caller may therefore retain access while a revocation has yet to apply. The operational handoff says a stuck tenant denies, whereas tracked action 54 records that earlier grants can continue authorizing. The collection freshness work in 16.4–16.5 addresses a different projection. **Disposition — discuss with Tenants, Platform and the Conversations authorization owner.** Align the architecture and handoff on the observed checkpoint guarantee, who supplies evidence of stalled delivery or restore, and what constitutes an acceptable denial or containment decision before production qualification. Retain the preserved tenant isolation obligation.
