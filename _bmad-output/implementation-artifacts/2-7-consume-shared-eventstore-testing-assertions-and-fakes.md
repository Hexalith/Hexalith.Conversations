### The assertion-strength tripwire (AC-2 / AC-4) — keep, don't weaken

`DomainResultAssertions` (shared) exposes `ShouldBeSuccess(result, expectedCount)`, `ShouldBeRejection(result)`, `ShouldBeNoOp(result)`, `ShouldContainEvent<TEvent>(result)`. These are coarse outcome checks. The Conversations aggregate tests assert **more**:

- Rejection paths: `result.Events.Single().ShouldBeOfType<ConversationRejectedDomainEvent>()` **plus field assertions** on the rejection (reason/code/message). `ShouldBeRejection` asserts only "is a rejection" → swapping **loses** the field checks. **Keep the direct assertions.**
- Success paths: `result.IsSuccess.ShouldBeTrue()` + `result.Events.Single().ShouldBeOfType<TEvent>()` (+ often payload-field assertions). Adopt `ShouldBeSuccess`/`ShouldContainEvent<T>` **only** where the test asserts exactly that and nothing more; keep direct assertions where payload fields / counts / ordering are checked.

Rule: a swap is allowed **iff** the shared helper asserts a superset (or exact equal) of what the direct code asserts. When in doubt, **keep the direct assertion and record the decision** — the story retires/weakens nothing.
